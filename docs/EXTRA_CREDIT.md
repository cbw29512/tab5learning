# Teacher-added content and extra credit — mandatory MVP
All teacher-authored/imported supplemental assignments are extra credit by default, distinct from approved core curriculum packages. Teacher configures nonnegative bonus weight per assignment, subject and grading period, optional aggregate cap, and whether combined grade may exceed 100. Zero weight is allowed. Unattempted extra credit adds zero and never reduces core grade. Additional adaptive practice is not automatically extra credit. Preserve separate core score, bonus awarded, combined classroom score and competency/mastery evidence. Supplemental completion earns badges; only designated major supplemental accomplishments may earn a clearly labeled certificate; no automatic core mastery or advancement from bonus points. A reviewed assessment can inform mastery only via the approved evidence policy, independent of extra-credit weight.
State/schema: assignment_id, content_id/version, source=teacher_supplement, student/class scope, subject_id, period_id, policy_id/version, maximum_bonus_points, reviewed earned/possible score, assessment status, teacher authorization, effective sequence and evidence references. Changes to weights or assessments append versioned audit records; original scores and historical report policies remain reproducible. No credit for uncertain/unreviewed or self-rated results until approved. Use decimal arithmetic, explicitly configured rounding and cap policy. No duplicate bonus per assignment/version; distinguish retakes from separate assignments. Teacher Studio previews effect before approving policy and displays core versus bonus separately. Learner sees optional extra challenges and achievements without penalty for skipping.
Acceptance: zero weight, missing submission, partial/full credit, duplicate/replayed assignment, correction and policy revision, invalid negative/nonfinite weights, unauthorized changes, cap on/off, separate subject/period scopes, unchanged core mastery and prior certificate history. Native UI, persistence and grading implementation pending.
