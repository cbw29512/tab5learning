# Four-year offline curriculum release contract

## Scope and release distinction

Shipping Version 1 includes enough reviewed core learning content for grades 1, 2, 3 and 4, not just examples or grade 4 enrichment. The engineering pilot proves the learning/teacher workflow and is not a shipping release. All core content and necessary assets must be supplied at initial delivery, accessible offline. Teacher additions are optional enrichment, not a dependency for completing the core path. Future grades 5+ remain expansion. Preserve the single learner, USB-only administration, camera and other existing acceptance gates.

Required subject coverage: mathematics; English language arts (reading, phonics where appropriate, comprehension, vocabulary, writing and spelling); science; social studies. The scope is educational support and practice aligned to reviewed standards, not a claim to replace school attendance, instruction, accreditation or formal promotion.

## Content and state schema contract

Each versioned catalog entry identifies grade label, subject, unit, skill, prerequisites, framework edition and standard identifiers, lesson/asset versions, estimated activity minutes, assessment/evidence requirements, accessibility features, provenance/license, educator review state and review date. Track coverage separately from learner progress. Preserve historic content references when packages change.

Per grade/subject, inventory all applicable reviewed standards and map each to instruction, guided practice, independent practice, assessment, remediation and review opportunities. Distinguish missing, drafted, reviewed, packaged and hardware-accepted content. Explicitly excluded standards require rationale and educator approval; do not count them as covered. Use original or lawfully redistributable content; standards alignment does not license copying third-party lessons.

## Pacing and assessment

Provide an educator-reviewed full-year learning sequence per grade, with flexible daily/weekly activities, sufficient distinct practice and assessments, remediation, spaced review and independent subject progression. Pacing is a proposed design choice, not a mandated calendar or a substitute for demonstrated mastery. Do not invent lesson-count targets before a standards inventory and educator review. Include narration, appropriate handwriting practice, flashcards and guided camera evidence within the existing activity contracts.

## Achievements and major accomplishments

Each grade/subject path includes frequent earned micro-achievements, progressively harder milestones and designated major accomplishment certificates. Candidate certificates include major verified skill/unit mastery, reviewed subject completion and grade-level learning-path completion. Award criteria and evidence policies must be reviewed by educators; distinguish completion from verified mastery. No formal grade promotion claim. Follow ACHIEVEMENTS.md for append-only evidence, idempotent issuance, nonblocking feedback, accessible presentation, correction handling, persistence and authorized Teacher Studio printing. The learner's portfolio persists throughout all four years and backup/restore. No rewards for mere clicks, time spent or purchases.

## Release evidence and gates

- Reviewed standards inventory and complete content mapping for all sixteen grade/subject cells.
- Original or licensed assets, answer keys, accessibility, educator approval and an explicit remediation/review path.
- Verified offline package installation and availability of all four years at delivery, without mandatory later downloads.
- Physical capacity budget for lessons/audio/images/models AND four years of retained progress/evidence under approved privacy and retention policy; benchmark real Tab5 with representative usage. Do not assume the 2 MiB math staging partition suffices for the entire curriculum or evidence.
- Tests for prerequisite/grade transitions, content versioning, missing assets, correction-driven recomputation, award issuance, reboot persistence, authorized backup/restore and printing.
- Child and teacher usability tests, including guided camera activity and verified USB-only operations.
- Teacher sign-off on educational sufficiency, coverage, progression rules and major certificate criteria.

Release remains blocked until these gates and all other MVP gates pass. Record evidence, exact revision, package sizes, test results and explicit remaining gaps in MASTER_TRACKER.md.

## Forward compatibility gate
Version 1 contains all reviewed grade 1–4 core packages at delivery. Architecture must permit later grade 5–12 packages and potentially college course outcomes without a four-grade schema ceiling or loss of prior evidence. Future curricula are not release blockers for Version 1. User-reported 512 GB microSD is the intended bulk storage target, subject to physical validation; measure actual package sizes and retention capacity rather than assuming a fixed allocation. See LIFELONG_LEARNING.md for constraints.
