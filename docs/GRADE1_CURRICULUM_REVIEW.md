## Proposed sequence, four quarters (flexible, not mandated pacing)
| Quarter | Mathematics | English language arts | Science | Social studies |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Counting, groups of ten, comparison, basic addition/subtraction | Sound awareness, letter/sound links, short vowels, decoding, print handwriting, simple stories | Sound and vibration; safe light observations | School/community roles, cooperation, citizenship, maps |
| 2 | Place value, composing numbers, equation meaning, story problems | Word building, syllable patterns, spelling, fluency, retelling, sentence formation | Materials and light; communication design challenge | SC location, map symbols, settlement types, landforms |
| 3 | Operation strategies, patterns, measurement, clocks, money | Further phonics, vocabulary, informational text, opinion and explanatory writing | Living things' external features, parent/young behavior, traits | Goods/services, economic choices, local/state government |
| 4 | Shapes, equal partitions, data collection, graph interpretation, cumulative application | Reading across genres, narrative writing, revision, oral presentation, cumulative decoding and comprehension | Sky patterns, seasonal daylight observations, cumulative investigation | Community change, sources, age-appropriate current events, civic inquiry |
Schedule revisit of all strands throughout the year; sky observations need multiple seasons. This is an original proposed scope, not a verified indicator inventory; precise numerical limits, phonics sequence, all subindicators and any missing topics must be checked against official editions. Adult/peer activities and physical investigations are essential when device-only answers cannot demonstrate a standard.

## Completion ledger schema
One row per applicable official indicator/subindicator and framework edition: source URL, page, official ID, concise paraphrased objective, grade, subject, applicable flag/rationale, ordered lesson IDs/revisions, each phase evidence reference, assets/rights, review/assessment plan, educator review decision/date/reviewer role, hardware acceptance evidence, open issues. Keep content coverage distinct from individual mastery. Never claim an indicator covered merely because its ID appears on a lesson or a child taps through it.
States: missing → drafted → educator-reviewed → packaged → hardware-accepted. Rejected/revision-needed is not counted as accepted. Exclusion requires educator rationale and cannot count as coverage. ## Definition of curriculum-complete
- Reconcile every in-scope indicator against the official document, with transparent crosswalks and no silent omissions.
- Each objective has actual age-appropriate instruction, worked examples, guided and independent practice, distinct assessment items, alternate remediation and later spaced review. Enough independent variants to avoid memorizing answer positions; sufficiency is teacher-reviewed, not an arbitrary lesson total.
- Include original or lawfully redistributable narrated stories, diagrams, manipulatives, handwriting/spelling exercises, word lists, experiments, and appropriately sourced maps/history; verify rights, accuracy, age appropriateness and accessibility. No external internet needed at runtime.
- Provide answer keys and rubrics that distinguish independent, prompted, uncertain and teacher-reviewed evidence. No automatic grading of unsupported oral or handwriting judgments.
- Provide coherent year-long sequence, prerequisite links, baseline and cumulative assessments, flexible pacing, meaningful rewards, evidence-backed mastery and revision history. Do not confuse completion, mastery and school promotion.
- All required lesson and asset revisions load offline on actual Tab5; signed USB install, persistence, rollback/recovery, battery and usability gates pass. Adult/peer activities documented clearly.
- Educator signs off each subject's coverage, practice depth, sequence, factual correctness, assessment validity, developmental fit, and known exclusions. No claim of accreditation or entire public-school program; health, PE, arts and other local requirements need separate scoping.

## Adult educator beta review
Give teacher a working offline device, populated four-subject draft library, coverage ledger and sample teacher records. Review with synthetic student identities only until privacy/consent/security gates. Ask teacher to inspect each lesson: objective/standard fit; difficulty and reading level; accuracy; pacing; feedback; accessibility; assessment fairness; missing practice; content gaps; activity feasibility. Rate accept / revise / reject / not reviewed, record lesson revision, objective ID, concrete issue, severity (blocking/important/minor), suggested fix and evidence. Re-review changed revisions. Teacher can critique platform navigation, touch, audio, screen layout, battery, assignment/review/correction/backup, and recovery independently of curriculum. Review readiness is not child beta approval.
