## Rules and sequence
- Missing: no plausible lesson candidate; partial_draft: related draft lesson(s), not verified coverage. Evidence IDs point to candidate activities, not certification of every activity. No exclusions or mastery claims. Review exact activity and phase relevance before acceptance.
- Close priority gaps first: math money, counting, patterns, place-value operations; ELA phonemic awareness, reading patterns, fluency, handwriting and writing; science authentic investigations and seasonal observations; SC geography, government and civic projects.
- For each row, produce independent variants, guided and independent assessment, remediation and spaced review. Verify rights and accessibility of media, teacher rubrics, and actual supported response mode. Keep reviewer/date and hardware evidence null until supplied; do not infer from CI.
- Reconcile official enumeration before any approved status. Add explicit test evidence, educator review, package and device acceptance separately. Coverage and mastery remain separate. Scope of commercial launch unchanged.
- No actual student records in this ledger. This does not authorize SD writes, USB provisioning, child beta, or firmware changes.
