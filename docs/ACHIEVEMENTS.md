# Learner achievements — mandatory MVP
My Learning Journey accessible from home. Views: current/in progress, completed, upcoming; distinguish ready for review, available, prerequisite locked and needs practice with text/icons. Show clear next step, evidence-based progress and personal trophy collection. Celebrate effort separately from verified mastery; no public ranking, punitive streaks, spending or time-based rewards. Age-appropriate appearance, optional celebration/audio and reduced motion.
State: versioned achievement definition tied to skill or activity goal, learner ID, evidence references, policy version, current criteria met/total, approved completion, append-only award/review history. Historical awards survive curriculum updates and corrections; current mastery may require review without shaming or deleting prior evidence. Progress counts criteria, not grade-equivalent scores or probability of mastery. Upcoming prerequisites visible. Host projection uses existing graph and policy results; it does not grant mastery. Native rendering, durable awards and integration remain required.
Acceptance: child opens journey in one tap, distinguishes current/completed/upcoming, identifies next action, sees progress change after verified evidence, sees prior awards after restart, and understands review without punitive messaging. Teacher can inspect supporting evidence; corrections update current status without deleting history. Synthetic tests only until privacy approval.
