# Grade 1 beta — release sequence

User prioritizes getting Pocket Learning into beta sooner by releasing grade-level curriculum packages incrementally. This is a controlled beta scope, not permission to omit security or hardware requirements.

## Milestones
1. Engineering integration: a complete offline math/teacher review and correction flow, using synthetic data.
2. Grade 1 closed beta: all four Grade 1 core subject paths (mathematics, ELA including reading/writing/spelling, science, social studies), sufficient reviewed teaching and practice material for the year, and all core device and teacher workflows.
3. Commercial launch: Grade 1 included with the device and separately purchasable future grades is the proposed alternative model. Final commercial scope, pricing, entitlement rules and purchase terms require user approval; until then the previous four-grade shipping requirement remains the commercial baseline.
4. Incremental grade packages toward Grade 12, with possible college extension. No grade-specific limits in the core learning engine.

## Grade 1 beta acceptance checklist
- Native offline single-child setup, persistent identity, age-appropriate navigation, touch, battery and restart acceptance. Persistent readable battery level/charge status, nonblocking low-battery warnings, unplug/replug and recovery tests are mandatory.
- Reviewed Grade 1 coverage across the four core subject areas: instruction, guided and independent practice, assessment, remediation and review; lawful offline assets.
- Mathematics and handwriting recognition/confirmation, reading/narration, spelling, flashcards, guided camera evidence activity.
- Easy teacher-authored flashcards, bulk AI-ready CSV authoring, teacher review, individual assignment and authenticated USB transfer.
- Reliable learning evidence and corrections, backup/restore, microSD capacity and interruption/recovery acceptance, no wireless sync.
- Evidence-based adaptive practice, frequent achievements, major accomplishment certificates and authorized teacher printing.
- Privacy, access controls, consent/retention policy, safe installation/recovery, physical and educator usability acceptance before children use real student data.

## Tracking and expansion
Track each beta gate by implemented, host-tested, hardware-tested and accepted evidence. Grade 2–4 content development does not block the Grade 1 closed beta. Do not imply the beta is ready or promise dates without measured evidence. Design signed/versioned offline curriculum packages now, preserve progress and certificates when adding later grades. Paid entitlements and payments are separate later commercial work; no mandatory cloud service or subscription in the learner. No pricing decision or payment integration is authorized by this document.

## Mandatory independent backup acceptance
Teacher can independently back up and restore authorized student progress and their own created flashcards, lessons and assets, including content versions and evidence links. Demonstrate recovery after microSD loss and replacement computer/device. See [TEACHER_BACKUP_RESTORE.md](TEACHER_BACKUP_RESTORE.md). No real student data before security and recovery acceptance.
